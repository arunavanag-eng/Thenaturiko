Where the store can sell more

From a walk through your collection page, product page, cart drawer and checkout.

01

Repeat food, no subscription

Cheela mixes and 21-day kits are bought again, but the product pages offer no way to subscribe.

02

No assistant on the page

Your FAQ answers protein, weight loss, kids and ingredients. A shopper has to find it, and there is nobody to ask.

03

A thin COD choice

Partial COD is ₹100, marked non-refundable, on a ₹2,356 order. It protects little and puts off a hesitant buyer.

04

No delivery date

Neither the cart nor checkout shows when the box arrives.

05

A cart that cannot open its upsells

A tap goes to the product page or does nothing, there is one header only, and the free gift is never shown.

06

Plus: recognise the shopper

What Fastrr adds on top: one-OTP login, a pre-filled address, and a way to reach visitors who leave before logging in.

06 · Shopper recognition

Know the shopper before they type

Shiprocket ships 7 lakh parcels a day, and up to 300M customers have tracked or ordered through it. That is what lets checkout recognise a shopper, fill the address and reach the ones who leave.

SSO LOGIN

One OTP, no account

Shoppers already in the Shiprocket network log in with one OTP and arrive recognised. SSO adoption is about 60% of eligible users.

ADDRESS PRE-FILL

98%+ in Tier 1, 90%+ in Tier 2–3

Addresses come from parcels actually delivered, corrected from delivery history. Above 95% overall.

ANONYMOUS VISITORS

Up to 45% identified

Visitors who never log in can still be identified, then followed up on WhatsApp. Up to 50–55% with login and checkout.
